For accountants & bookkeepers

Your client's mileage log, live.

MileTracker users can share a private read-only link to their log. Open it in any browser to see every trip, the totals and the deduction. No account, no app, no attachments.

How the live link works

Three steps, all on your client's side.

01

Your client creates a link

In MileTracker: Settings → Accountant access. They can add your name or email as a label so they know who has it.

02

You open it in a browser

You see the trips from the last 13 months: date, start time, from and to, miles, category, purpose, deduction, tolls, parking and vehicle. Filter by month or category, print it, or download a CSV.

03

It stays current

New trips your client logs show up on the same link. No more chasing year-end PDFs.

Privacy, by default

Your client stays in control.

  • Personal trips are hidden unless your client turns that off.
  • Links expire after 180 days. Your client can create a new one any time.
  • Turning the link off deletes the shared copy of the trips from our server.
  • No sign-up for anyone. The link itself is the access, so share it like a password.

Details are in our Privacy Policy.
