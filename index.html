Questions

Good to know

Does it drain my battery?

MileTracker uses motion sensors to wake GPS only when you start driving. You can choose Economy, Balanced or Rocket tracking in Settings to trade precision for battery.

Is the log IRS-ready?

Each trip records the date, start and end place, distance and the purpose you choose, close to the time of the drive. That is what the IRS asks a mileage log to show. Export a PDF, CSV or Excel file whenever you need it.

What happens to personal trips?

Mark them Personal with one tap. They are left out of deduction totals and can be left out of reports and the accountant link.

Can my accountant see my log?

Yes, if you want. Create an accountant link in the app and your accountant can view and download your trips in a browser, with no account. You can turn the link off at any time. How the live link works.

How does the free trial work?

Premium starts with a 7-day free trial through the App Store. Cancel in your iPhone's subscription settings before the trial ends and you won't be charged.
